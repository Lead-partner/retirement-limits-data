# Sources

All of the following were read on **13 November 2026**. Official PDFs and HTML pages are linked; this repository does not redistribute the IRS or CME files.

## Contribution limits

- [IRS, COLA increases for dollar limitations on benefits and contributions](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/cola-increases-for-dollar-limitations-on-benefits-and-contributions) — web table for 2023–2026, including the 2026 IRA, 401(k), SIMPLE, and catch-up figures, and links to the annual notices.
- [IRS, Prior Years COLA Tables PDF](https://www.irs.gov/pub/foia/ig/tege/cola-table-dci005ma4578824.pdf) — historical COLA series used for IRA limits 2002–2025, § 402(g) (where the PDF has a year column), § 414(v), § 415(c), and SIMPLE limits through 2025. This edition has no 1990 column and does not go back through 1987–1988.
- [IRS Notice 2025-67](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/n-25-67.pdf) — official 2026 cost-of-living adjustments (published with IR-2025-111).
- [IRS IR-2025-111, 13 November 2025](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/401k-limit-increases-to-24500-for-2026-ira-limit-increases-to-7500) — news release announcing the 2026 401(k), IRA, TSP, and catch-up figures.
- [IRS, Retirement topics — IRA contribution limits](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/plan-participant-employee/retirement-topics-ira-contribution-limits) — 2023–2026 IRA limits including the age-50 totals.
- [IRS Publication 590-A (2025)](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p590a.pdf) — "For use in preparing 2025 Returns," catalog 66302J, dated 15 January 2026. Historical IRA worksheet (1997–2024), What's New for 2025 ($7,000 / $8,000), and What's New for 2026 ($7,500 / $8,600).
- [TSP, Historical information on contribution limits](https://www.tsp.gov/hr-payroll-resources/historical-info/) — official TSP table of § 402(g), § 415(c), and catch-up limits, 1987–2025. This is the source for 1987–1988 and 1990 (years the IRS COLA PDF in this edition does not tabulate), and the source for recording TSP catch-up as N/A in 2002.
- [TSP Bulletin 25-3, 19 November 2025](https://www.tsp.gov/bulletins/25-3/) — 2026 TSP elective-deferral, catch-up, and annual-additions limits.

Where the IRS COLA PDF and the TSP historical table overlap (most years 1989 and 1991–2025), the elective-deferral and § 415(c) figures match.

## Required minimum distributions

- [IRS Publication 590-B (2025), Appendix B, Table I](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p590b) — “Single Life Expectancy (For Use by Beneficiaries),” ages 0–120 and over. The values were extracted and verified from the IRS page on 13 November 2026. Spot checks: age 0 = 84.6, age 55 = 31.6 and age 57 = 29.8 (also quoted in worked examples), age 120 and over = 1.0.
- [26 CFR § 1.401(a)(9)-9, Life expectancy and Uniform Lifetime tables](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-1/subject-group-ECFR6f8c3724b50e44d/section-1.401(a)(9)-9) — eCFR text of Table 1 in paragraph (b) (Single Life Expectancy, the same table as Publication 590-B Table I) and Table 2 in paragraph (c) (Uniform Lifetime Table). The eCFR is authoritative but unofficial.
- [T.D. 9930, 85 Fed. Reg. 72472 (12 November 2020)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2020-11-12/html/2020-24723.htm) — official Federal Register publication of the current tables, applicable for distribution calendar years beginning on or after 1 January 2022.

## IRA-eligible metals

- [26 U.S.C. § 408(m)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/408) — collectible-distribution rule and the coin and bullion exceptions, via the Legal Information Institute unofficial official-text publication.
- [CME Group COMEX Rulebook Chapter 113, Gold futures](https://www.cmegroup.com/rulebook/COMEX/1a/113.pdf) — "Gold delivered under this contract shall assay to a minimum of 995 fineness."
- [CME Group COMEX Rulebook Chapter 112, Silver futures](https://www.cmegroup.com/rulebook/COMEX/1a/112.pdf) — "Silver delivered under this contract shall assay to a minimum of 999 fineness."
- [CME Group NYMEX Rulebook Chapter 105, Platinum futures](https://www.cmegroup.com/rulebook/NYMEX/1a/105.pdf) — "Platinum delivered under this contract shall be a minimum of 99.95% pure."
- [CME Group NYMEX Rulebook Chapter 106, Palladium futures](https://www.cmegroup.com/rulebook/NYMEX/1a/106.pdf) — "Palladium delivered under this contract shall be a minimum of 99.95% pure."
